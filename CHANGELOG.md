## v1.1.0

- New: the raid filter picks the difficulty and which raids to show, so Blizzard's search box is free for words in group names (try "skip" or a boss name).
- New: an option to sort raid groups furthest along first.
- New: a quick list beside Blizzard's listing window. One click lists your key as Mythic+ with your last playstyle; for a party member's key, type its level in the title first (it reminds you).
- New: a playstyle filter (Learning, Relaxed, Competitive).
- New: "Leader timed it at my level" keeps groups whose leader has timed the dungeon at your best key there or higher.
- New: group tooltips show every member's name and the leader's best run in the dungeon; raid tooltips list every member by role.
- New: players who left groups early lately show Blizzard's leaver mark, in the list and when leading.
- New: an option to hide groups that declined you, until you reload.
- Groups new to your list have a mint name, with a quick shimmer when the list is short (Options can turn it off).
- The left edge on your sign-ups shows how they went: mint while waiting, amber when declined, grey for other endings.
- Clean-up is now one switch in Options; hover it to see what it hides.
