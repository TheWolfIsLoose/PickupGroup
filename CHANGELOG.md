## v1.0.0

PickupGroup's first full release.

- New: **a chime when your group fills.** When a dungeon group you joined through a sign-up reaches five, a short tone plays. Turn it off in Options.
- New: **your rejection record.** Sign-up history now leads with how many times you've been turned away (since a date you can reset), today's tally, your fastest "no" and your longest dry spell, with a running commentary. `/pug stats` puts it in chat.
- Sign-up history tells sign-ups the game withdrew when you got into another group ("Moved on") apart from ones you cancelled, and marks keys that ended in a vote to abandon ("Bailed").
- The filter panel now opens with the list, so your filter is ready without a click.
- A line in chat on login shows the version you're running.
- New icon, and tidier colors and wording throughout.
- Detailed recording for bug reports is now off unless you switch it on with `/pug debug`.
